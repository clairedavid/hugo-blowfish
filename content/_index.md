I am a curious mind eager to fiddle with particle detectors, play with data, and share the beauty of physics, algorithms, and my latest adventures in nature.

I trained as an engineer, then switched to fundamental science, contributing to the Large Hadron Collider at CERN.

In 2023 I took a leap in the middle of my tenure track to pursue two interests at once: learning the math behind machine learning, and teaching in Africa. I eventually resigned my post to relocate to Cape Town and help launch a new master's in AI for science.

There I found a new passion: mentoring. After colliding particles, I can't resist colliding talented students with scholars.

> "Claire's superstrength is to connect people." — Max Welling

Curious about my research, teaching, talks, or my recent cycling journey across Canada? Reach out below. I'm open to teaching missions, a physics-ML project, a field trip to help climate scientists, or any adventure I haven't thought of — with or without a bike.
