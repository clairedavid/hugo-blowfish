== PAGE HEADING ==
Life of a Particle: stochastic modelling in particle physics

== TEXT ==

From its birth in the heart of the Large Hadron Collider’s detectors to its propagation and — spoiler alert — death in readout material, the course follows the journey of a particle and introduces stochastic modelling techniques with numerous hands-on sessions in Python.
