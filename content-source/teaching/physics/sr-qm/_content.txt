Introduction to Special Relativity & Modern Physics

== TEXT ==

In this introduction to the theories of relativity and quantum mechanics, the relativistic concepts of space, time and energy are explained and the quantum nature of radiation and matter is discussed.


## Topics

| Special relativity | Modern physics |
| --- | --- |
| Before Einstein<br>Basics of special relativity<br>Twin paradox and Doppler effect<br>Relativistic dynamics | Experimental basics of quantum theory<br>The particle nature of radiation<br>Towards a theory of the atom<br>Matter waves |
